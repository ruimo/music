# Liner Notes: Edvard Grieg – Lyric Pieces, Op. 68: No. 3, "At Your Feet"

Edvard Grieg’s *Lyric Pieces* (*Lyriske stykker*) serve as the ultimate diary of his musical soul. Spanning ten volumes and forty years of his life, these short solo piano works are celebrated for their ability to paint vivid, deeply personal pictures. Among these miniature masterpieces, **"At Your Feet" (Op. 68, No. 3)** stands out as a breathtaking testament to Grieg’s gift for capturing profound human intimacy.

### The Essence of Devotion

Published in 1898, "At Your Feet" (*Zu deinen Füßen*) is a work of quiet intensity. The title itself evokes a physical gesture of absolute surrender—be it in love, prayer, or a plea for forgiveness. Grieg translates this vulnerability into a rich, late-Romantic musical language that feels both deeply private and universally resonant.

### Musical Characteristics

*   **A Yearning Theme:** The piece opens with a tender, questioning melody that seems to sigh. Grieg uses rising intervals that stretch upward, only to fall back gently, mimicking the cadence of a heartfelt confession.
*   **Late-Romantic Harmony:** This work is highly chromatic, filled with unexpected harmonic shifts and expressive suspensions. The shifting tonal colors create a sense of bittersweet longing, a characteristic hallmark of Grieg’s mature style.
*   **The Emotional Arc:** While the piece begins in a hushed, contemplative whisper, it gradually builds to a passionate, surge-like climax. Here, the melody breaks free with intense yearning before slowly subsiding, dissolving into a series of serene, prayer-like chords that bring a sense of profound peace.

### Why It Captivates

What makes "At Your Feet" so enduringly appealing is its exquisite restraint. Grieg avoids empty virtuosity or theatrical grandiosity. Instead, he invites the listener into a quiet space. The music does not perform; it whispers. 

For the listener, it offers a moment of pure, unfiltered emotional honesty—a three-minute sanctuary of warmth, devotion, and gentle grace.
